About

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. from the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur . I am working under the guidance of Dr. Debapriya Basu Roy . My research area includes Hardware Accelerator Design and Hardware Security.

During my Ph.D., I work on hardware implementation and acceleration of Post-Quantum Cryptographic algorithms. My work also includes Physically Unclonable Functions (PUFs) and hardware security protocols.

I completed my M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from University of Kalyani . My thesis was supervised by Dr. Anirban Mukhopadhyay and Dr. Sujoy Chatterjee .

I also worked as a JRF at NIT Durgapur on secure lightweight FPGA architectures under Dr. Bibhash Sen .
