About

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. from the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at IIT Kanpur. I am working under the guidance of Dr. Debapriya Basu Roy. My research area includes Hardware Accelerator Design and Hardware Security.

During my Ph.D. I am working on Hardware implementation and acceleration of Post Quantum Cryptographic Algorithms. I have also worked on Physically Unclonable Functions (PUFs) and hardware security protocols.

I completed my M.Tech from University of Kalyani. My M.Tech thesis was supervised by Dr. Anirban Mukhopadhyay and Dr. Sujoy Chatterjee.

I worked as JRF at NIT Durgapur on FPGA security research.
