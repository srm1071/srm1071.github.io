About

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Kanpur. I work under the guidance of Dr. Debapriya Basu Roy. My research focuses on Hardware Accelerator Design and Hardware Security.

My work includes hardware implementation and acceleration of Post Quantum Cryptographic algorithms. I have also worked on Physically Unclonable Functions (PUFs) and hardware security.

I completed my M.Tech from University of Kalyani. My M.Tech thesis was supervised by Dr. Anirban Mukhopadhyay and Dr. Sujoy Chatterjee.

I worked as a JRF at NIT Durgapur on FPGA security architecture research.
